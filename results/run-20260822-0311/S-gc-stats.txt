=== the control: no collector at all
  c, c++, rust, fortran, swift and the two Python targets have no
  garbage collector on this path. Their allocation after startup is
  zero by construction, which is what the rows above converge to.

Reading: a collection count in single digits over 200 ticks, and a total
GC time under a millisecond, mean the collector is idle. Whatever these
numbers say about the runtimes, they do not say it about their
collectors -- so docs/RESULTS.md should not, either.
